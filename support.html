Frequently Asked Questions

Is the game free?

Yes. The game is free to download and play. Ads may be shown during gameplay.

How do I remove ads?

You can permanently remove ads via a one-time in-app purchase. The purchase is linked to your Apple ID.

I purchased Remove Ads, but ads are still showing

Please try restoring purchases from the app settings or restart the app. If the issue persists, contact support with your Apple ID and device model.

Does the game collect personal data?

The app does not collect personal information such as your name or email. Advertising and usage data may be processed by third-party services in accordance with the Privacy Policy.

Is there a timer or penalties?

No. The game is designed to be calm and relaxing, without timers or pressure.

On which devices is the game supported?

The app supports iPhone and iPad devices running a recent version of iOS.
