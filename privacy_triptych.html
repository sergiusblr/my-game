Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Triptych (the “App”) handles data.

1. Contact

Siarhei · Email: skraiko@gmail.com

2. About the App

Triptych uses the iPhone camera to capture photos for diptychs and triptychs. It stores originals, project information, editing settings and thumbnails inside the app's own storage on your device. Originals let you reopen, edit and export a project. The app also keeps preferences such as layout, guides, borders, automatic saving and haptic feedback on the device.

3. Permissions and Sharing

Camera permission enables capture; your iPhone manages this permission. Triptych requests permission to add a finished composition to Photos when you save one manually, or when you finish a new composition with automatic saving enabled. You can keep using the internal gallery even if Photos access is denied.

When you choose Share, Triptych prepares a JPEG of the finished composition and opens the system share sheet. The service you choose there handles the shared copy under its own terms. A copy saved to Photos is managed by the Photos app. Device backup and Photos sync follow your iPhone and Apple account settings.

4. Data Collection

Triptych does not collect personal data. The app has no user accounts, no developer-operated servers, and no tracking, advertising or analytics SDKs. The app provider never receives your photos, projects or settings.

5. In-App Purchases

Triptych offers a single, one-time, non-consumable purchase ("Triptych Pro") that removes a watermark shown after a free usage limit. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit; we do not collect, store or process any payment information.

6. Deletion and Retention

You can delete a saved project inside Triptych at any time; this removes its local project and originals from the app. Deleting Triptych removes all of its app data from that installation. Copies already saved to Photos, included in a device backup, or shared with someone else can still exist there — manage those copies through the corresponding service or your device settings.

7. Your Rights

Since Triptych keeps everything locally on your device and the provider never receives your data, there is nothing for us to access, export or delete on your behalf beyond what you already control in the app itself. Contact us via email with any privacy questions.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time.
