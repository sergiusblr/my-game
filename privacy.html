Privacy Policy

Number Match: Pairs or Ten (Android package com.sudoku.pairor10, formerly listed as “Not Sudoku: Logic Puzzle”)
Developer: Kupala Games (Siarhei Kraiko), Vilnius, Lithuania
Effective date: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data the mobile game Number Match: Pairs or Ten (the “App”) by Kupala Games (“we”, “us”) accesses, collects, uses and shares, and what choices you have. It applies to the Android version of the App distributed through Google Play.

1. Who we are and how to contact us

The App is developed and published by Kupala Games, an independent developer based in Vilnius, Lithuania. Kupala Games is the data controller for the limited processing described here.
Privacy contact: sergiusblr@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 30 days.

2. Summary

  • You can play without an account. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or other personal details.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run our own servers and do not receive this data.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob, not Kupala Games, may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve and measure ads, as described below.
  • Optional in-app purchases (for example, removing ads) are processed by Google Play Billing. We never see your payment details.

3. Data stored on your device

The App saves your level progress, game state, tutorial status, settings (such as sound and vibration) and, if you made a purchase, whether ads are removed. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us. It is deleted when you clear the App’s data or uninstall the App.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK users)

  • Consent: personalized advertising and related use of device identifiers. You give or refuse consent through the UMP form.
  • Legitimate interests: non-personalized advertising, fraud prevention, security and ad measurement, which keep the App free.
  • Performance of a contract: processing in-app purchases you request.

6. Your choices

  • Ad consent: where required, the App shows a consent form before ads load. You can change your choice later from the App’s settings/privacy options, where available, or by clearing the App’s data.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Google > Ads).
  • Remove ads: an optional in-app purchase turns ads off.
  • Offline play: the game works without an internet connection. No ads are requested while offline.

7. Data retention and deletion

We do not store your personal data on our own servers, so we hold nothing about you that we could keep or delete. Game data on your device stays there until you clear the App’s data or uninstall the App. Data collected by Google AdMob, UMP and Google Play is kept and deleted by Google according to its own retention policies (see How Google retains data). The App has no user accounts, so there is no account to delete. If you email us, we use your message only to reply and delete it once your request is resolved, unless the law requires us to keep it.

8. Security

The App does not send your game data anywhere. Communication between the App and Google services uses encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). We do not request sensitive Android permissions such as location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or US state laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because we do not hold personal data on our servers, most requests are best handled through the controls above or directly with Google. You can still contact us at sergiusblr@gmail.com and we will help. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In Lithuania, this is the State Data Protection Inspectorate (VDAI).

10. Children

The App is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the App, contact us at sergiusblr@gmail.com and we will help get it deleted.

11. International transfers

Google may process data in countries outside your own, including outside the EEA/UK. Google relies on appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy, for example when the App’s features or the SDKs it uses change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the changes are significant, we will also tell you in the App or on its Google Play page.
