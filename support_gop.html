Frequently Asked Questions

Is the game free?

Yes. The game is free to download and play. Ads may be shown during gameplay.

How do I remove ads?

You can permanently remove ads via a one-time in-app purchase. The purchase is linked to your Apple ID.

I purchased Remove Ads, but ads are still showing

Please try restoring purchases in the app settings or restart the app. If the issue persists, contact support with your device details.

Does the game have timers or pressure?

No. Grain of Peace is designed to be calm and meditative — no timers, no scores, no stress.

How do I play?

Sort grains by color using simple interactions — tap or drag depending on the mode. Enjoy the flow and satisfying movement.

Does the app collect personal data?

The app does not collect personal information such as your name or email. Some usage and advertising data may be processed by third-party services.

Which devices are supported?

The app supports iPhone and iPad devices running recent versions of iOS.
