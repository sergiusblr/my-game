Privacy Policy

Last updated:

1. Who we are

Developer: Not Sudoku: 10 in a Square (indie developer). Contact: your-email@example.com

2. What this app does

The game displays ads using Google AdMob. In certain regions (EEA/UK/CH), a consent form powered by Google UMP SDK is shown before any ads are loaded.

3. Data used for ads

Depending on your consent and device settings, AdMob may process:

  • Advertising ID, device identifiers
  • Approximate location, app interactions, crash logs
  • Ad performance metrics

Learn more at Google’s policies for partners and AdMob documentation.

4. Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Consent — for personalized ads and certain data uses in EEA/UK/CH.
  • Legitimate interests — for basic app operations, security and fraud prevention.

5. Your choices

  • In-game: open Settings → Privacy / Ads preferences to review or change consent.
  • Device settings: reset Advertising ID / limit ad personalization.
  • Regional rights (GDPR/UK): request access/erasure by emailing us.

6. Children

This game is intended for a general audience. If we become aware of data collected from children without appropriate safeguards, we will delete such data.

7. Data retention

We do not store personal data on our own servers. Ad-related data is handled by Google (AdMob/UMP) according to their retention policies.

8. Transfers

Ad services may process data on servers outside your country. Google applies appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

9. Contact

Email us at your-email@example.com for privacy questions or requests.
