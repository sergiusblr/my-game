1. Data Controller & Contact

Privacy contact: sergiusblr@gmail.com

2. What the App Does

The App is a casual number-puzzle game. It is free to download. Users may optionally remove ads permanently via a one-time in-app purchase.

On Android: The App is monetized through advertising provided by Google AdMob. For users in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, we display the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form before any ads are loaded, where required by law.

On iOS: The App is also monetized through Google AdMob. Before loading ads or using data for tracking, we request your permission via Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework (system dialog on first launch). The in-app purchase “Remove Ads” is available on iOS to permanently disable ads.

3. Categories of Data & Purposes

On Android and iOS (where the App shows ads), the following may apply:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID, or on iOS the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) when you allow tracking): ad personalization, ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and aggregated measurement.
  • Usage data (such as ad impressions, interactions, and aggregated gameplay events): analytics, ad performance measurement, and service improvement.

On iOS, we request your permission via ATT before using data for tracking; you can allow or deny in the system dialog. If you deny, ads may still be shown in a non-personalized form.

We do not collect names, email addresses, or other direct contact details inside the App.

In-app purchases (such as the optional one-time purchase to remove ads) are processed directly by the Apple App Store. We do not receive, store, or process payment or billing information.
