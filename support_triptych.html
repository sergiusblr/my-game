Frequently Asked Questions

Is the app free?

Yes. Triptych is free to download and your first 10 completed compositions export without any watermark. After that, a small "Triptych" watermark appears on exports until you unlock Triptych Pro with a one-time purchase.

What does Triptych Pro remove?

A one-time, non-consumable purchase that permanently removes the watermark from every export, including compositions you made before purchasing.

I purchased Triptych Pro, but the watermark is still showing

Open Settings in the app and confirm your purchase status, or restart the app. If the issue persists, contact support with your device model and iOS version.

Does Triptych collect personal data?

No. Triptych has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. Everything — your photos, projects and settings — stays on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I save an original photo instead of just the composition?

Triptych keeps every captured original inside the app's internal gallery so you can reopen and re-edit a project later. Use the Share or Save action to export the finished diptych or triptych to Photos.

Which devices are supported?

Triptych is an iPhone app that requires a recent version of iOS.
